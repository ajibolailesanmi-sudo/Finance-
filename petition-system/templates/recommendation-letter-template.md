# Recommendation Letter Template

> **This is a draft for the named recommender to review, revise, and adopt in
> their own name.** It must not be presented as independently authored without
> the recommender's genuine review and signature. Vary structure and wording
> across letters — identical letters are an RFE trigger.

```
Recommender: <Full name, title, institution>
Status: [ ] Independent (no prior relationship)  [ ] Dependent (co-author/advisor/colleague)
```

---

**[Letterhead / date / USCIS address block]**

**1. Who I am and why my opinion matters**
The recommender's credentials, position, and standing in the field — establishing
expertise to evaluate the beneficiary's work. `[NEEDS RECOMMENDER INPUT: ...]`
for anything not on record.

**2. How I know of the beneficiary and their work**
Basis of knowledge. For independent experts: how they came to know the work
(publications, conferences, adoption) despite no personal relationship — this is
what gives the letter weight.

**3. The specific contribution and its impact**
The heart of the letter. Describe one or two **specific** contributions and the
**concrete impact** — who adopted, cited, built on, or was affected by the work,
and why it matters to the field. Avoid generic superlatives.

**4. Tie to the governing standard**
- EB-1A: original contributions of major significance; sustained acclaim.
- EB-1B: internationally recognized as outstanding.
- EB-2 NIW: national importance of the endeavor and the beneficiary's positioning
  to advance it.

**5. Closing endorsement**
A clear, personal statement of support.
