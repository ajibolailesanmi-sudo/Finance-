# Petition Letter Template

> Choose the section set matching the category. Replace all `<...>`. Every factual
> assertion must cite an exhibit, e.g. `(Exhibit 7)`. Insert `[GAP — needs: ...]`
> where the record is missing evidence — never fabricate to fill it.

---

## Header (all categories)
```
<Date>
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
Re: Form I-140 Immigrant Petition — <Category>
Petitioner: <self / employer name>
Beneficiary: <Beneficiary full name>
```

---

## EB-1A — Extraordinary Ability

**I. Introduction and summary of acclaim**
Brief statement of who the beneficiary is, the field, and the thesis that the
evidence establishes sustained national/international acclaim.

**II. The beneficiary and the field**
Context on the field and the beneficiary's standing within it.

**III. Regulatory criteria** *(argue each claimed criterion in its own subsection)*
For each: quote the regulatory language → present the evidence with exhibit cites
→ explain why it satisfies the plain language. Target 4+ clearly-met criteria.

**IV. Final merits — sustained acclaim and top of the field**
Totality argument (Kazarian Step 2): why the evidence as a whole shows the
beneficiary is among the small percentage at the very top.

**V. Conclusion** — request approval under the preponderance standard.

---

## EB-1B — Outstanding Professor / Researcher

**I. Introduction** — beneficiary, field, and the petitioning employer.
**II. Threshold requirements** — (a) international recognition as outstanding,
(b) 3+ years qualifying experience (cite employment letters), (c) the qualifying
permanent/tenure-track offer (cite the offer and the institution's standing).
**III. Regulatory criteria** — argue at least 2 of the 6, each with exhibit cites.
**IV. The position and employer** — permanence and the institution's reputation.
**V. Conclusion.**

---

## EB-2 NIW — National Interest Waiver

**I. The proposed endeavor** — define it *specifically* (what, method, sector).
**II. EB-2 base eligibility** — advanced degree (with evaluation) or exceptional
ability.
**III. Prong 1 — Substantial merit and national importance** — argue broader
field/industry/public implications, not geography.
**IV. Prong 2 — Well positioned to advance the endeavor** — education, record of
success, plan, progress, third-party interest (exhibit cites).
**V. Prong 3 — Benefit to waive the job-offer/labor-cert requirement** — genuine
balancing argument.
**VI. Conclusion.**
