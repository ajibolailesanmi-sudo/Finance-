## 4. "Would-be RFE questions" (as USCIS might phrase them)

1. **National importance / evidence.** Submit objective documentary evidence from
   an authoritative source substantiating the scale/national significance of the
   problem, and explain how the beneficiary's *specific* endeavor — as distinct
   from the general field of ML/climate — has national importance under *Dhanasar*.
2. **Adoption — independent proof & scope.** Submit independent evidence from each
   adopting entity describing actual operational use, scale, and impact. Clarify
   which adopters are in the U.S., and explain how a foreign pilot establishes
   national importance *to the United States*.
3. **Well positioned vs. merely qualified.** Submit evidence distinguishing the
   beneficiary's *influence on and progress within* the field from qualifications
   common to many competent researchers, with field-relative context for citation
   metrics.
4. **Independent expert testimony.** Submit evidence from experts with no personal,
   academic, employment, or collaborative relationship, and the objective basis
   (specific published work relied upon) for each assessment.
5. **Grant role / experience.** Clarify the beneficiary's specific role/share on
   the NSF collaboration given co-PI (not PI) status, and reconcile stated years
   of experience with the documented education/employment dates.
